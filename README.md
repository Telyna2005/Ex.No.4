# **Scenario-Based Report Development: Prompting Techniques Evaluation**

## **Scenario**

You are a researcher evaluating how different prompting techniques affect AI-generated insights for a **“Smart Healthcare Monitoring System using IoT.”**
The aim is to analyze how various prompt designs lead to distinct quality, depth, and coherence of responses.

---

## **1. Comparative Analysis Prompt**

### **Prompt Example**

> Compare the advantages and limitations of using IoT in Smart Healthcare Monitoring Systems with respect to data accuracy, patient privacy, and cost efficiency.

### **Outputs**

**🔹 ChatGPT Output:**

> IoT in Smart Healthcare Monitoring provides real-time patient data collection, improving diagnostic accuracy. However, sensor calibration and network instability may affect data accuracy. Privacy remains a concern as continuous data transfer increases vulnerability to breaches. Although initial setup costs are high, long-term operational savings and reduced hospital visits make it cost-effective.

**🔹 Gemini Output:**

> IoT systems improve healthcare by enabling constant monitoring and early detection. Yet, privacy threats from unsecured devices and high maintenance costs remain barriers. Data accuracy is influenced by device quality and environmental interference.

### **Evaluation**

| Criteria      | ChatGPT                                | Gemini                       |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Depth**     | Detailed with multi-faceted comparison | Moderate, more general       |
| **Structure** | Clear separation by factors            | Compact, lacks structure     |
| **Accuracy**  | High                                   | Medium                       |
| **Overall**   | ✅ **Better analytical insight**        | Suitable for short summaries |

---

## **2. Experiential Perspective Prompt**

### **Prompt Example**

> Imagine you are a patient using an IoT-based healthcare device at home. Describe your experience and challenges while interacting with the system.

### **Outputs**

**🔹 ChatGPT Output:**

> Using my IoT device gives me peace of mind. It tracks my heart rate and reminds me to take medication. However, sometimes the app disconnects, making me anxious. Sharing my health data with doctors remotely feels reassuring, but I still worry about privacy leaks.

**🔹 Gemini Output:**

> My IoT device monitors my vitals and alerts my doctor instantly. It’s convenient, but the frequent false alarms frustrate me. I also wish the interface was simpler.

### **Evaluation**

| Criteria    | ChatGPT                               | Gemini                     |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Empathy** | Deep emotional and sensory connection | Practical tone             |
| **Clarity** | Clear and relatable                   | Short and direct           |
| **Realism** | High                                  | Moderate                   |
| **Overall** | ✅ **More immersive storytelling**     | Good for brief reflections |

---

## **3. Everyday Functioning Prompt**

### **Prompt Example**

> How does an IoT-based Smart Healthcare Monitoring System impact the daily workflow of doctors and nurses?

### **Outputs**

**🔹 ChatGPT Output:**

> Doctors receive real-time data dashboards, reducing manual checks. Nurses can prioritize critical patients via automated alerts. This minimizes paperwork and allows better time management. However, data overload sometimes causes alert fatigue.

**🔹 Gemini Output:**

> IoT helps medical staff track patients remotely and reduces manual data entry. Still, too many notifications can distract healthcare workers.

### **Evaluation**

| Criteria              | ChatGPT                         | Gemini           |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| **Practical Insight** | Detailed role-based workflow    | Summary-style    |
| **Comprehensiveness** | Covers pros & cons              | Partial coverage |
| **Readability**       | High                            | High             |
| **Overall**           | ✅ **Better operational detail** | Basic overview   |

## **4. Universal Prompt**

### **Prompt Example**

> Explain IoT-based Smart Healthcare Monitoring Systems in simple terms that anyone can understand.

### **Outputs**

**🔹 ChatGPT Output:**

> Imagine your health devices—like watches or patches—sending updates about your heartbeat or temperature to doctors automatically. That’s IoT in healthcare. It helps doctors monitor your health without hospital visits and can even warn you early if something’s wrong.

**🔹 Gemini Output:**

> IoT healthcare means devices that send your health data to doctors using the internet. It helps doctors track you from anywhere.

### **Evaluation**

| Criteria       | ChatGPT                        | Gemini              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------- |
| **Simplicity** | Excellent analogy use          | Clear but basic     |
| **Engagement** | Friendly and vivid             | Informative only    |
| **Clarity**    | Very high                      | High                |
| **Overall**    | ✅ **Best educational clarity** | Minimal explanation |

## **5. Structured Prompt Refinement**

### **Prompt Example**

> Structure your response under three headings:
> **1. Working Principle**, **2. Benefits**, **3. Limitations** of IoT-based Smart Healthcare Monitoring Systems.

### **Outputs**

**🔹 ChatGPT Output:**

> **1. Working Principle:** IoT devices collect physiological data via sensors and send it to cloud servers for analysis.
> **2. Benefits:** Enables early diagnosis, continuous monitoring, and reduced hospital visits.
> **3. Limitations:** Data security, connectivity issues, and high maintenance cost.

**🔹 Gemini Output:**

> **Working:** IoT gathers and sends health data.
> **Benefits:** Saves time, improves patient care.
> **Limitations:** Privacy risk, cost, and device errors.

### **Evaluation**

| Criteria         | ChatGPT                        | Gemini                    |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| **Organization** | Highly structured              | Structured but concise    |
| **Detail Level** | Rich                           | Limited                   |
| **Suitability**  | Research report                | Quick overview            |
| **Overall**      | ✅ **Ideal for formal writing** | Compact version for notes |
